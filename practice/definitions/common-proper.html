Common vs Proper Nouns

Understanding general labels versus specific identities.

Start Practice Quiz
General

Common Noun

"A word that refers to a non-specific person, place, or object."
— Linguae Learning, Udemy

A common noun is a general name for any person, place, or thing. It isn't specific to one individual entity.

e.g., city, phone, teacher
Practice More
Specific

Proper Noun

"A word that refers to a specific person, place, or object."
— Linguae Learning, Udemy

A proper noun is the specific name of a particular person, place, or thing. It identifies one unique entity.

e.g., Paris, iPhone, Mr. Smith
