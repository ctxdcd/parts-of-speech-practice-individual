Countable Nouns

"A countable noun becomes plural by adding -s at the end of the word (e.g., 1 chair, 2 chairs)."
— Linguae Learning, Udemy

A countable noun is a word that can be counted and has both singular and plural forms.

e.g., 3 mice, 10 men, 4 chairs

Uncountable Nouns

"You never add -s to them; they only exist in the singular form."
— Linguae Learning, Udemy

An uncountable noun refers to things that cannot be counted. They only have singular forms.

e.g., sugar, dirt, milk
